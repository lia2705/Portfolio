About me

Hi, I’m Efthalia - but you can also call me Lia :)

I’m a UX designer with a background in Mobile Media, where I learned to bridge design and implementation. During my studies, I worked on real digital products in both academic and industry settings, gaining practical experience in user-centered design, research and prototyping.
I’m especially passionate about XR and exploring how immersive technologies can shape future experiences.

I love blending design, technology and visual storytelling to create meaningful products!
